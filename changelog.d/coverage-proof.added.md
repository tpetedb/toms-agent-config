`just coverage-proof` (`tac proof coverage`) derives every condition the harness claims from the configuration and the source (each hook check per wired client, every pipeline stage and gate, handoff template and contract, memory command, human kind and transition, the four enforcement layers, every doctor check, C1 to C6 and the section 9 bypass attempts), holds `dev/coverage/map.toml` to that set, runs the mapped tests and writes `dev/out/coverage.json` bound to the tested revision. `--list` prints the condition ids.
