`just dev-proof` (`tac proof dev`) runs the ledger's verify and schema drift gates, the bypass attempts of `tests/test_bypass.py` and the mutation runs of `tests/test_mutation.py` (each hook check per client removed from the render, from the checker and from the guard in turn), then prints `SKIP live: <probe>: <reason>` for every C1, C6 and GitHub-bound live probe whose requirements are not met and writes `dev/out/dev-proof.json` bound to the tested revision; `--require-live` exits non-zero on any skip. The live probe kinds are accepted by the probes schema and refused by name by the runner until it observes them.
