Every stage-to-stage prompt now goes through a declared template and contract: `contracts/handoffs/` holds the thirteen payload contracts in the OpenAI strict subset, `templates/handoffs/` and `templates/human/` the prompts that read and write them, and `contracts/envelope.schema.json` the envelope. `tac handoff render` checks the payloads a template reads, renders its prompt in a sandbox and writes a dispatch envelope binding the template, the contract and the prompt by sha256; `tac handoff validate` refuses a result without that envelope, gives a failing result one repair pass and then turns it into a pending human item.
